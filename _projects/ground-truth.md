Before Fervo Energy rang the Nasdaq bell, it was a flood in Houston, a Stanford
class project, and a young engineer convinced fracking technology could crack
geothermal energy wide open.

*Ground Truth* traces Fervo's decade-long climb from that grad-school hunch to
a multibillion-dollar IPO, told not as corporate history, but as a human one.
New to the company myself, I talk to the founders and early team about the
years nobody believed them, the breakthrough in the Nevada desert, and the
largest next-generation geothermal project on Earth rising in Utah.
