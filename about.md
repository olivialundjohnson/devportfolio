I'm a storyteller and energy nerd interested in big ideas and how they shape
the world around us. I started my career in media, working across film,
television, and documentary before moving into climate and energy. Today, my
work spans creative production, energy research, policy, and communications.

I'm interested in the space between technical ideas and the people they
affect—and in finding compelling ways to make complex subjects feel tangible,
relevant, and human.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me swimming, chasing snow, or deep in a book.
